How we build

Design Process

We follow the engineering design process (EDP). Every subsystem on our robot goes through the same six steps of the VEX Engineering Design Process, from the first idea to the version that goes to competition. Our approach is data-driven and uses the EDP: we record all of our data and use it to make our decisions.

01

Identify the problem

Define the challenge, the requirements and the constraints before any design.

02

Brainstorm solutions

Come up with several ideas and prototypes, and compare them against the requirements.

03

Select the best solution

Pick the strongest idea, explain why, and plan it out in CAD.

04

Build and program

Build from the CAD model and program it, all in C++.

05

Test the solution

Set pass and fail criteria first, then test and record all the results.

06

Repeat and improve

Use test and scrimmage results to improve the design, then go through the process again.

The Team

We are four passionate students from Caution Tape Robotics in Markham, Ontario, taking on the 2026–27 Level Up season with one goal: to win the VEX World Championship.

Off the field

How we bond

A good alliance starts with trusting each other. The night we brought the banners home, we went bouldering together: spotting each other, cheering every send and working out routes together, the same way we work through a robot problem.
